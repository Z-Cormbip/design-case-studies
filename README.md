### 🎨 Rethinking Design Fundamentals

When I used to see a good design — whether it was a post on social media or a structure in my surroundings — I’d just think, *“That looks cool,”* and move on. My appreciation stopped there. I once thought modern aesthetics were bland, dull, and too stiff. How wrong I was.

Through studying design fundamentals, I started to understand the **psychology behind good design** — why balance, rhythm, and spacing make something feel right. It also made me reflect on my background as an illustrator, where I often stayed in my comfort zone. This time, I learned to break away from that safety and explore new compositions with intention.
